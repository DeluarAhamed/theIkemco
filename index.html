The practice

Most clients are not looking for an agent. They are looking for someone they can hand something to and stop thinking about.

I built this practice inside two industries that run on trust and on privacy. Athletes and entertainers do not need another person selling to them. They need one person who understands what a career actually looks like from the inside: the timing, the liquidity, the travel, the exposure, and the people who have to be kept out of it.

So the work looks less like real estate and more like advisory. I read the contract. I sit with the business manager. I talk to the attorney. I tell you when the answer is no, or later, or somewhere else entirely. Then I execute quietly and completely.
