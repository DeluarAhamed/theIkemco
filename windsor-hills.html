Windsor Hills

The same ridge, the same history, and a market where four sales can set the tone for a year.

03

The read

Windsor Hills shares the ridge and much of the architecture with View Park, and often the same buyers. Tenure here is long. Families hold for decades, pass property down, and stay. The result is a market that moves on very few data points.

For a buyer, patience is the strategy and relationships are the edge. For a seller, preparation is everything, because the pool of buyers is small, informed, and has been watching this street for years.

What I watch for

Comparable sales that are eighteen months old. In a market this thin, stale comps mislead both sides of the table.
