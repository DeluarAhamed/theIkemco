Why this corridor

These five neighborhoods sit along a single ridge in the middle of Los Angeles, and they have been undervalued by the wider market for most of the last fifty years. That is changing, but slowly enough that there is still real value in knowing which street, which block, which side of the hill.

Inventory is thin across all five. Families hold for decades. A meaningful share of what trades never appears publicly, which means the buyer pool is effectively decided by who the seller trusts. That is not a market you win with a search alert.

I work here because I know it, and because the clients who ask me about Bel Air and Malibu are often surprised to learn what their money does twenty minutes east.
