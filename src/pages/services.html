01

Residential Advisory

Buying and selling the home, handled like a transaction that matters.

Primary homes, second homes, guest houses and the privacy driven purchase that has to be structured before anyone knows it is happening. Most of my residential work sits between $1 million and $10 million, and a meaningful share of it never appears on a public site.

On the buy side that means bringing you properties before they list and telling you honestly which ones are wrong. On the sell side it means preparation, pricing that reflects the actual buyer pool rather than the neighbor's fantasy, and a marketing decision made with you about how visible you want to be.

Your business manager and attorney are in the conversation from the beginning. Nobody who protects you should be learning about a decision after it has been made.
