Home/The Approach

Four things that do not change.

Whatever the price, whatever the city, whatever the deadline. This is the standard, and it is the reason clients come back with the second and the third transaction.

01

Discretion first

Names stay out of it. Many of my transactions never touch the open market, and the ones that do are structured so that your name is not the story. Trusts and entities are set up with your attorney before an offer goes out, not after a public record appears.

02

One point of contact

You will not be routed to a team member. I show the property, I write the offer, I sit in the negotiation, I attend the inspection and I am at the final walkthrough. If you call at nine on a Sunday, you are calling me.

03

Your advisors in the room

Business managers, attorneys and financial advisors are brought in early, not handed a signed contract to react to. The people who protect you should never learn about a decision after it has been made.

04

After the close

Management, contractors, insurance, refinance timing, the next acquisition. The relationship is the product. The transaction is a moment inside it, and it is rarely the last one.

The process

What working together actually looks like.

05

First, a conversation. Thirty minutes, no listing presentation. You tell me the shape of what you are trying to do and what has to stay private. I tell you honestly whether I am the right person for it.

Then, the plan. Written, short, and shared with your business manager and attorney. Target areas, structure, budget, timing against your contract or your production calendar, and the exit if the plan changes.

Then, the quiet search. Most of what I bring you will not be on a public site. That part takes calls, not clicks, and it is where the relationships actually pay.

Then, execution. Offer, negotiation, inspections, appraisal, the difficult phone calls. I handle the parts that are uncomfortable so you do not have to be in them.

Then, the long part. Management, vendors, improvements, refinance timing, the next purchase. Most of my clients are still clients years after their first close.
