How it works

Ground up builds, full renovations and the targeted pre sale work that actually moves a number. Builder selection, budget review, permit expectations and site oversight, so the project runs on a schedule someone other than the contractor believes in.

Most cost overruns are visible in the first three weeks of a project, in the contract and the allowances, long before anyone breaks ground. That is when I want to be involved.

What you get

•

Builder selection

Bids compared line by line, references called, and the questions asked that owners do not know to ask.

•

Budget and allowances

Where the six figure surprises hide, and the three line items worth arguing about before you sign.

•

Permit reality

An honest read on timelines, especially inside a historic district or on a hillside lot.

•

Pre sale improvement

The short list of work that returns more than it costs, and the long list that does not.
