Neighborhoods

Five communities, covered the way a resident would cover them.

These are not search pages. They are what I would tell you over coffee about where the value actually sits, which streets trade, and what a buyer should be watching for.

01

Ladera Heights

Ladera sits in the pocket between Culver City and the Baldwin Hills ridge, which is why buyers who work on the Westside keep circling back to it. Mid century floor plans on generous lots, quiet cul de sacs and a long history of owners who simply do not sell.

Inventory is thin by design. When something good comes up it is often placed rather than listed, which is exactly the kind of transaction I am built for.

02

View Park

View Park is a listed historic district, and it shows. Spanish revival, Tudor and period modern homes on curving streets with a downtown skyline view that buyers do not expect the first time they drive up.

Historic designation protects the character and complicates renovation. Knowing which changes will clear review and which will not is the difference between a smart purchase and a two year delay.

03

Windsor Hills

Windsor Hills shares the ridge and the history with View Park, and often the same buyers. Tenure here is long, families hold for decades, and the result is a market where three or four sales can set the tone for a year.

For a buyer, patience is the strategy. For a seller, preparation is everything, because the pool is small and informed.

04

Baldwin Hills

The ridge holds some of the best views in the city and the Village Green, a national landmark that trades on its own logic entirely. Below it, a mix of post war stock and newer construction that rewards anyone willing to look past the first block.

Proximity is the underrated asset here. Clients who commute to studios, downtown or the airport figure that out in a single afternoon of driving.

05

Leimert Park

Leimert Park is the cultural center of Black Los Angeles and one of the few neighborhoods in the city where the architecture, the plan and the community all arrived together in the 1920s and largely survived.

The new rail stop has changed the conversation. So has a wave of buyers who want to be part of the place rather than merely near it. Income property here deserves a serious look.

Services

Beyond the transaction.

Sports and Entertainment

Representation shaped around a career, not a calendar. Trade windows, production schedules, signing timelines and the privacy requirements that come with a public name. Relocation is handled end to end, including schools, security review, staff and the parts nobody thinks about until week one.

Commercial Real Estate

Income property, land, and owner user buildings for clients converting earnings into something durable. Underwriting reviewed alongside your business manager, with an honest read on hold periods, tenancy risk and what the asset actually returns after the story is stripped out.

Property Management

Stewardship for homes and income property while you are on location, on the road or simply elsewhere. Vetted vendors, scheduled maintenance, tenant relations and one monthly report that tells you what happened and what it cost.

Construction Advisory

Ground up builds, full renovations and the targeted pre sale work that actually moves a number. Builder selection, budget review, permit expectations and site oversight, so the project stays on a schedule that someone other than the contractor believes in.
