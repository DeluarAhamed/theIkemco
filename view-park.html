View Park

A listed historic district with a skyline view, and the strictest design review in the corridor.

02

The read

View Park is on the National Register, and it shows. Spanish revival, Tudor and period modern homes on curving streets, with a downtown view most buyers do not expect the first time they drive up the hill. The housing stock has been cared for by families who have held it for generations.

Historic designation protects that character and complicates renovation. Knowing which changes will clear review and which will not is the difference between a smart purchase and a two year delay. I have walked clients through both outcomes and I will tell you honestly which one you are buying.

What I watch for

Anything the previous owner added without permits. In a historic district that is not a small problem to unwind.
